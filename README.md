# Apple (AAPL) Stock Market Analysis — 2020–2025

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an exploratory **stock market analysis of Apple Inc. (AAPL)** using historical market data from 2020 to 2025.

The analysis was developed using **Python** and the **yFinance** library to retrieve and analyze historical stock market data.

The project explores Apple's stock price movements, trading volume, returns, and overall performance over the selected period.

The objective is to demonstrate practical skills in **financial data analysis, time-series analysis, data visualization, and Python-based data analytics**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The analysis was designed to:

- Retrieve historical Apple stock data
- Examine stock price movements over time
- Analyze daily and historical returns
- Investigate trading volume patterns
- Identify periods of significant price movement
- Visualize Apple's historical market performance
- Apply exploratory data analysis techniques to financial data

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Historical Price Analysis

The project examines Apple's historical stock prices to understand how the stock price changed throughout the analysis period.

Price trends were visualized to identify periods of growth, decline, and increased market movement.

---

### 2. Daily Return Analysis

Daily stock returns were calculated to examine the percentage change in Apple's stock price from one trading period to the next.

This provides a better understanding of short-term price movements and volatility.

---

### 3. Trading Volume Analysis

Trading volume was analyzed to identify periods of unusually high or low market activity.

Changes in trading volume can provide additional context when examining significant price movements.

---

### 4. Price Range Analysis

The analysis examines Apple's daily:

- Opening price
- Highest price
- Lowest price
- Closing price

This provides a broader view of daily market activity and price fluctuations.

---

### 5. Time-Series Visualization

Historical stock data was visualized using charts to identify:

- Long-term price trends
- Short-term fluctuations
- Periods of increased volatility
- Changes in trading activity
